Part 2 Natural Beauty Topic 01
題目 關鍵字 擬答 重要句型
01 / NATURAL BEAUTY
01

題目

Describe a place in your country that is famous for its natural beauty.
You should say:
  • where that place is
  • what kind of place it is
  • what makes it beautiful
  • and explain why this place is famous in your country
02

關鍵字

Alishan · Chiayi mountain area mountain views altitude increases wide variety of plants & trees green forests huge old trees sunrise sea of clouds golden light Alishan Forest Railway fresh mountain air forest scenery peaceful & relaxing international tourists
03

Band 6.5–7 擬答

Uses the closest available en-GB female voice on your device.

I would like to talk about Alishan, a famous mountain area in Chiayi, Taiwan. It is a very popular place for people who enjoy natural scenery and mountain views.

What I really like about Alishan is its wide variety of plants and trees. One interesting feature is that the scenery gradually changes as the altitude increases. There are beautiful green forests and many huge old trees, which give the whole area a peaceful and relaxing atmosphere.

When I visited Alishan, I had already heard a lot about its sunrise and the famous sea of clouds. However, the view was even more beautiful than I had expected. I had been looking forward to seeing it for a long time, so I got up very early in the morning. As the sun slowly came up, the clouds below gradually turned golden. It was definitely one of the most impressive views I had ever seen.

Another thing I really enjoyed was taking the famous Alishan Forest Railway. The little train went through the forest, and along the way, I could enjoy the fresh mountain air and the forest scenery around me.

I think Alishan is famous because it offers beautiful scenery, a peaceful atmosphere, and a unique railway experience all in one place. That is why it has become one of the most popular destinations for both Taiwanese people and international tourists.

04

重要句型

It is a very popular place for people who … 簡單自然地介紹「這是一個很受某類人歡迎的地方」，比直接用 be popular with 更好說出口。
What I really like about … is … 自然帶出「我最喜歡的特色是……」，很適合 Part 2 展開描述。
One interesting feature is that … 用 that 子句介紹特色，可以增加句型變化，又不需要使用困難單字。
… gradually changes as the altitude increases. 用 as 表示「隨著……」，很適合描述環境、景色或情況逐漸改變。
…, which give the whole area a … atmosphere. which 關係子句可用來補充前面事物帶來的效果，讓描述更完整。
I had already heard a lot about … 過去完成式：到「實際去阿里山」之前，我已經聽過很多相關資訊。
… was even more beautiful than I had expected. 比較級搭配過去完成式，適合描述實際體驗超出原本預期。
I had been looking forward to … 過去完成進行式：強調某個期待在過去持續了一段時間。
Another thing I really enjoyed was … 非常實用的延伸句型，用來加入第二個體驗或亮點。
… offers … all in one place. 用 offers 取代 has，搭配 all in one place，能讓總結更自然也更有層次。
That is why … 完整形式的原因總結句，適合收尾，也符合這份筆記不使用縮寫的練習原則。
